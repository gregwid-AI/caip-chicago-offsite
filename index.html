3Cloud content & takeaways

  • Cognizant + 3Cloud model: 3Cloud said Cognizant is positioning it as the Microsoft service arm and Microsoft relationship lead for Cognizant globally, while keeping the 3Cloud brand and Microsoft-first go-to-market.
  • MSA / account paper: the 3Cloud session called out that Cognizant currently has paper on 99 of roughly 200 accounts in scope and offered to map that list to the team structure to make collaboration easier.
  • Funding path: 3Cloud described a partner-led advisory / roadmap investment motion that can move early-stage discovery quickly and can be stacked with Microsoft programs.
  • Factory motion: their team stressed explicit task mapping and regular syncs with Microsoft when Cloud Accelerate / factory motions are in play so customer workstreams do not collide.
