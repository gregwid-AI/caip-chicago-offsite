Aug 15–16, 2026 · North Avenue Beach · Free

One of the largest free air shows in the US. Jets, aerobatics, Navy Blue Angels. If you’re arriving early, don’t miss it.

Free · Beach

Lollapalooza ↗

Grant Park · First weekend of August

May just be wrapping up or freshly passed. Check the lineup — might be worth extending your trip.

Music

August · Citywide

August is prime dining season. Many top restaurants offer prix-fixe menus — great excuse to try somewhere special.

Dining

Chicago Architecture River Cruise ↗

Running daily · 75-min cruise down the Chicago River

One of the best ways to see the city. Highly recommended for first-timers and veterans alike.

Free · 2 min walk
